# Genopets

> Genopets is a free-to-play, move-to-earn NFT game built on the Solana blockchain that gamifies an active lifestyle. Players nurture a digital pet — a Genopet — that evolves from real-world physical activity (step tracking), then battle, craft, stake, and trade in-game NFTs and Habitats. The economy pairs the GENE governance token with the in-game KI utility token earned by moving. Genopets is a consumer mobile game and does not publish a public developer API.
